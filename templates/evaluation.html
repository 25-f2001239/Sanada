Methods note: why dense mixtures are hard & why speaker splits matter

Counting is done by a CNN over one 8-second log-mel spectrogram. As more sources are added, their spectra overlap in time and frequency, so individual sources stop being separable features and the spectrogram looks like broadband noise. Because all 13 speakers in Org_* appear across standard train/val/test splits, we also evaluate a strict Held-Out-Speaker split (holding out 3 unseen speakers during training) and report it above as the honest unseen-speaker generalisation metric.
